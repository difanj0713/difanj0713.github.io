Why this matters.

Back in 2023, serious text classification still usually meant a task-specific BERT-style model or fine-tuning general-purpose models separately for each task. Prompting was convenient, but task-specific classifiers remained the stronger default. SPIN showed that the missing signal was already inside: intermediate layers held clean, separable evidence that the final layer left behind. Reading that evidence improved every pretrained comparison, brought the strongest unchanged models close to published state-of-the-art results, and still added another gain after fine-tuning.

The core choices

What. Which. Where.

SPIN comes down to three choices: what signal to keep, which view to read, and where to stop. Together, they define the classifier.

01 · Sparsify

Keep the signal. Drop the crowd.

η is the share of a layer’s classifier weight SPIN wants to preserve. Neurons are ranked by influence, and SPIN keeps the smallest group whose combined weight reaches that target.

η ≈ 0η = 0.8

At η = 0.4, the paper selects about 3% of neurons per layer. Even at η = 0.8, fewer than one in four are needed—the classification signal is concentrated.
