Two sources of safety evidence

Harmful content and unsafe tool use require different evidence.

Harmful content is expressed directly in a request or action. Unsafe tool use depends on whether an action is consistent with its authorization, tool schema, and preceding context.

Matched training data

Internal readout complements full fine-tuning.

With identical backbones, training trajectories, and evaluation folds, the frozen probe matches full fine-tuning on Qwen3-4B and slightly exceeds it on Llama-3.1-8B. Applying the same readout after fine-tuning improves both results, showing that internal readout and safety fine-tuning are complementary.

Training and serving efficiency

No label decoding and no backbone gradients.

Agent-SIREN scores a selected internal state directly. In the measured 4B comparison, it has the lowest single-stream latency, while fitting the probe takes substantially less time than full safety fine-tuning.

Early exit

Early exit at layer 24.

The validation-selected safety state occurs before the final layer. Stopping at layer 24 retains 99.8% of the result and increases measured throughput by 43%.
